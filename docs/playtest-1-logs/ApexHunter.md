## Summary of problems (prioritised)

1. HIGH (balance) Big foundings starve en masse: 87 → 8 in 20 days (1st design) and 89 → 7 (2nd design); there's no carrying-capacity/food feedback on the design page, and founding in a summer drought kills via starvation + heat "exposure".
2. HIGH (balance/design) A design inside a wild species' body-plan gap becomes a hybrid swarm: at 1,485 animals only 10.5% of my lineage's alleles were mine (64.9% wild fox, 24% GeneBroker). The per-lineage cap (1,500) still counts those hybrids. Invisible to the player until the "Whose genes" panel.
