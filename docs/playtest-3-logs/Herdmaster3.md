## Summary for the persona
Herbivore swarm works: egg-laying (litter 6), hibernating, estivating, obedient grazer with 4 groups and territories reached 1,900 animals and won Era 1. Group commands mattered most for (a) getting groups onto fresh grass+water each spring and (b) holding them there with territories. Weak points: big-group migrations "arrive" immediately (arrival radius = rally radius up to 24 tiles), herds walk home when the 15-day settle ends, territories never warn when their water or grass runs out, and every summer/autumn drought plus every winter (hibernation toll) cuts the herd by 30-70%.
